All structural quantities are measured directly on the cached graphs actually used by the benchmark suite ($N=60$, 10 seeds), not transcribed by hand. Only SNAP Higgs Twitter (REAL public data) derives from a public dataset; the other 5 networks are synthetic surrogates that reproduce the qualitative topology of their reference sources. $r$ is the directed reciprocity $2\,\#\text{mutual}/|E|$, $Q_0$ and $K_0$ are the modularity and the number of communities of the Louvain partition of the initial topology; $K_0$ is the quantity that bounds $Q$ via $Q \leq 1-1/K$ (Proposition~1).
